# Math Quest Legends

A pixel-art adventure for Kindergarten to Grade 5 in the style of classic handheld RPGs. You explore with your buddy, solve wild Legends' math puzzles to calm them down, and make friends along the way. It's a sister game to Math Quest and uses the same math.

## How to play

- Pick a **buddy** at Professor Pi's lab: Sproutle, Emberkit or Splashfin. Your buddy follows you everywhere and helps with every kind of math. Turn around and press A (or MENU → Talk to buddy) to practice 5 puzzles together on the skill that needs it most, hear how you're doing (your strongest skill, what's improving, what's next), or get a tip. Sproutle, Emberkit and Splashfin each have their own way of talking. Any Legend on your team can be your buddy: MENU → Team → pick one → Make buddy.
- Walk through the tall grass on Route 1. Each wild Legend asks one kind of math puzzle:

  | Puzzles | Route 1 | Sunny Shore | Dusty Canyon | Frostpeak | Glowcap Cave |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Adding | Hootsum | Tallytoise | Cactotal | Pengplus | Batsum |
  | Taking away | Snipsnap | Gulpgull | Minusnake | Meltling | Molemin |
  | Numbers (counting, comparing, place value, rounding) | Pebblit | Starbig | Lizardo | Yetiny | Crystling |
  | Groups (skip counting, doubles, times) | Buzzlet | Octogroup | Arraydillo | Flurrfox | Webbit |
  | Sharing (parts, halves, division, fractions) | Shareling | Jellishare | Coyshare | Harefrost | Glowsplit |

  Every kind of puzzle has math for every grade, so every Legend shows up at every grade.

- In battle, choose **SOLVE**. A right answer powers your buddy's move, and a fast first try gives extra power.
- **Answers:** in Grades 1–5 you type answers (Grade 5 gets a decimal point key). During a question, the D-pad and buttons turn into a big number keypad (typing an answer builds memory better than picking one). Kindergarten picks from big choices. Grown-ups can change this in MENU → Settings → Answers.
- Solve enough puzzles and the wild Legend **calms down**. Legends you solved on the first try often want to be your friend and join your team.
- Each area has a boss guarding the way north. Beat them to earn five badges:

  | Area | Boss | Badge |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Route 1 (meadow) | Ranger Rowan | Sum Badge |
  | Sunny Shore (beach) | Captain Marina | Wave Badge |
  | Dusty Canyon (desert) | Sheriff Sol | Mesa Badge |
  | Frostpeak (snow) | Coach Ivy | Frost Badge |
  | Glowcap Cave (crystal cave) | Keeper Onyx | Crystal Badge |

  Every boss opens with the Legend for the skill you need most. Wild Legends get stronger in each new area.
- **53 Legends:** 3 buddies, 30 in Pinecrest Valley and 20 in Whisperwood. Every area has a Legend for adding, taking away, numbers, groups and sharing, plus a boss partner.
- **Map:** MENU → Map shows the world and lets you quick-travel to any place you've visited.
- **Worlds and the Portal Shrine:** Pinecrest Valley is World 1 (Route 1 to Glowcap Cave). Beating Keeper Onyx wakes a portal to World 2, and the Portal Shrine, a ring of standing stones down the path west of Pinecrest Town, has a portal to all five worlds. Sleeping portals are dark until the world before them is beaten. Worlds 3–5 (Brightport City, Coral Deep, Sky Isles: working names) are still being built.
- **World 2, Whisperwood:** Whisper Forest, Mirror River, Bogwater Swamp, Treetop Trail and Thunder Falls, with 20 new Legends and five bosses (Warden Fern, Fisher Finn, Marsh Mae, Scout Wren, Elder Brook). A portal by the forest entrance goes back to the shrine.

- **Battles:** the two Legends face each other, bounce on their toes and blink now and then.
- **Boss houses:** every boss guards the door of a log cabin, striped tent, stone tower or domed hall. Rest Centers have a heart on the roof, Professor Pi's lab has a π badge, and your home has a teal roof and a chimney.
- **Badge colors:** each badge's color shows the grade it was won at: Bronze (K), Silver (1), Gold (2), Emerald (3), Sapphire (4), Crystal (5). Start a harder grade and every boss offers a rematch to upgrade its badge.
- The Rest Center (pink roof) heals your team. If your whole team needs a rest, you wake up there. Nothing is lost.

**Controls:** on-screen D-pad, A, B and MENU buttons, or the keyboard: arrows/WASD to move, Z or Space for A, X for B, Enter for the menu. During a question, type the number and press Enter (or 1–4 to pick a choice), and press B or H for a hint.

## Hints

The buddy helps the way Math Quest's tutor does:

1. **💡 Hint:** a short strategy tip ("Make a ten!", "Think addition: 7 + ? = 15") and a picture: ten-frame, base-ten blocks, array, number line or fraction bars.
2. **A second try:** after a wrong answer, a hint about the likely mistake ("Careful! This one is taking away, not adding.", "Did you remember to borrow?").
3. **🦉 Teach me:** a step-by-step lesson (count on, make a ten, count back, column adding, borrowing, skip counting, friendly fives, think multiplication, rounding on a number line, split and multiply, long division, matching fraction bottoms, lining up decimal points and more). From Grade 1 the buddy then asks "Which trick did we use?", and a similar problem follows to try yourself. After a kid has seen a lesson twice, it skips the first worked step.

## The math

Skills, number ranges and difficulty follow Math Quest's grade scheme (Common Core K–5). Each grade has all five kinds of Legend math:

| Grade | Adding | Taking away | Numbers | Groups | Sharing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kindergarten | within 10 | within 10 | counting, which is more | counting on, by 10s | parts of a number (5 = 2 + ?) |
| Grade 1 | within 20 | within 20 | comparing, tens and ones | doubles | mystery numbers (6 + ? = 13) |
| Grade 2 | two-digit | two-digit | place value, skip counting | equal groups | halves |
| Grade 3 | within 1,000 | within 1,000 | rounding | times tables | division facts |
| Grade 4 | to 10,000 | to 10,000 | rounding big numbers | multi-digit × one digit | dividing by one digit, equal fractions |
| Grade 5 | decimals | decimals | decimal place value | two-digit × two-digit | long division, adding fractions |

Also from Math Quest: weak skills come up more often, mastered skills stretch to bigger numbers, 15% of problems review earlier grades, Grades 2–4 include short story problems, and missed problems come back after 1, 3 and 7 days.

**Research behind it:** typed answers use retrieval practice; review after 1, 3 and 7 days is spaced practice; wrong-answer hints give specific feedback; pictures (ten-frames, blocks, arrays, number lines, fraction bars) come before symbols; Teach me uses worked examples that fade as a kid gets familiar; number lines build number sense (Siegler & Booth); asking which trick was used prompts self-explanation; and praise names the strategy that worked, not the child ("Making a ten worked!").

On top of that, wild encounters follow the player's needs: while one skill is clearly weak, extra encounters bring the Legend that practices it. As the skill improves, that Legend shows up at higher levels with bigger numbers.

## For grown-ups

- **Grade:** set at the start and changeable any time in MENU → Settings.
- **Read aloud:** off by default. Turn it on in MENU → Settings to have the game read dialog, questions and hints out loud. The 🔊 button reads the current question at any time.
- **Voice:** the game picks the most natural-sounding voice on the device, and MENU → Settings → Voice lets you choose another. For the best sound, install a high-quality voice:
  - iPhone/iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, then download a voice marked "Enhanced" or "Premium" (for example Ava or Zoe).
  - Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices, then download a Premium or Enhanced voice.
  - Windows: Microsoft Edge has natural-sounding online voices built in (for example Aria or Jenny).
  - Chrome on any computer includes Google voices, which sound better than the basic system ones.
- **Progress:** MENU → Grown-ups shows first-try accuracy and mastery for each skill, which skill is being practiced most, and how many missed problems are waiting for review.
- Progress is saved on the device (browser storage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Save files:** MENU → Save & load → Export save file downloads (or shares) a backup. Load save file brings it back on any device, and the title screen has Load save file too.
- **Devices:** works on phones, tablets and computers. On iPad and other tablets it scales up. Turned sideways, the controls move to a grip on each side of the screen, like a Nintendo Switch. MENU → Settings → Controls can keep them on the sides or below the screen all the time (this setting stays on the device, not in save files).

## Running it

It's a plain static site with no build step. Serve the folder and open it in a browser:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then go to http://localhost:8000. It also works offline after the first visit, and it can be added to a phone's home screen.

To publish it, push to GitHub and turn on GitHub Pages for the `main` branch (Settings → Pages).

## Files

- `index.html`, `style.css`: the page and the handheld-console look
- `js/data.js`: creatures, moves, people and maps
- `js/art.js`: all pixel art, drawn in code
- `js/math.js`: the math engine: skills per grade, adapting, hints and Teach me lessons
- `js/game.js`: movement, the following buddy, dialog, menus, battles and saving
- `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `*.png`: offline support and app icons
- `tools/icons.html`: draws the home screen icon options from the game's art
- `tools/qc.html`: quality checks for Legends, maps, math problems, hints, reading level and offline files
- `tools/layout-qc.js`, `tools/flow-qc.js`: in-game checks for question layout and the hint / Teach me path

**Quality checks:** serve the folder, open `tools/qc.html` and read the report (it runs about 33,000 checks). For the in-game checks, open the game with `?dev`, then in the browser console run `await import('./tools/layout-qc.js'); await layoutQC()` (try phone and tablet sizes) or `await import('./tools/flow-qc.js'); await flowQC()`.

**Dev unlock:** open the game with `?dev` in the address (for example https://samw90.github.io/MathQuestLegends/?dev). MENU → Settings then shows *Dev: unlock everything* (all badges, places, Legends and portals, plus a level 30 team) and *Dev: go to Portal Shrine*. Unlocking changes the save, so export it first to keep a copy.

For testing, open the game with `?dev` in the address to get `MQL.ask(MQL.MathEngine.make('sub-100', 2))` in the browser console, or `?dev&quiz=sub-100&grade=2` to jump straight into practice questions (handy on a phone or simulator).
