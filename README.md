# Math Quest Legends

A pixel-art adventure for Kindergarten to Grade 5 in the style of classic handheld RPGs. You explore with your buddy, solve wild Legends' math puzzles to calm them down, and make friends along the way. It's a sister game to Math Quest and uses the same math.

## How to play

- Pick a **buddy** at Professor Pi's lab: Sproutle, Emberkit or Splashfin. Your buddy follows you everywhere and helps with every kind of math. Turn around and press A (or MENU → Talk to buddy) to practice 5 puzzles together on the skill that needs it most, hear how you're doing (your strongest skill, what's improving, what's next), or get a tip. Sproutle, Emberkit and Splashfin each have their own way of talking. Any Legend on your team can be your buddy: MENU → Team → pick one → Make buddy.
- Walk through the tall grass on Route 1. Each wild Legend asks one kind of math puzzle:

  | Puzzles | Route 1 | Sunny Shore | Dusty Canyon | Frostpeak | Glowcap Cave |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Adding | Hootsum | Tallytoise | Cactotal | Pengplus | Batsum |
  | Taking away | Snipsnap | Gulpgull | Minusnake | Meltling | Molemin |
  | Numbers (counting, comparing, place value, rounding) | Pebblit | Starbig | Lizardo | Yetiny | Crystling |
  | Groups (skip counting, doubles, times) | Buzzlet | Octogroup | Arraydillo | Flurrfox | Webbit |
  | Sharing (parts, halves, division, fractions) | Shareling | Jellishare | Coyshare | Harefrost | Glowsplit |

  Every kind of puzzle has math for every grade, so every Legend shows up at every grade.

- In battle, choose **SOLVE**. A right answer powers your buddy's move, and a fast first try gives extra power. Solving also keeps your Legend ready, so it takes less of the next hit: wild Legends are gentle (half the hit), while trainers, Remy and bosses fight like in Pokémon (moves as strong as yours, a quarter off when you solved it). A low-level team can lose to a boss, so it pays to train up and make friends first.
- **Answers:** in Grades 1–5 you type answers (Grade 5 gets a decimal point key). During a question, the D-pad and buttons turn into a big number keypad (typing an answer builds memory better than picking one). Kindergarten picks from big choices. Grown-ups can change this in MENU → Settings → Answers.
- Solve enough puzzles and the wild Legend **calms down**. Legends you solved on the first try often want to be your friend and join your team.
- **Route trainers:** 2 or 3 trainers stand beside the way through every area (Campers, Picnickers, Hikers, Swimmers, Skiers and Miners). Walk into one's line of sight and they spot you ("!"), walk up and battle with 1 or 2 Legends from the area. Beaten trainers share a math tip when you talk to them. They're placed from each map automatically: beside the shortest walk to the boss, never blocking a path.
- **Stamp card and the boss gate:** a boss only battles once you've made 3 friends among the area's Legends. MENU → Stamp card (Professor Pi's) shows each area's Legends in this world: friends in color, seen ones in gray, and ✓ once the boss is open (★ once beaten). Bosses you've already beaten are always open.
- Each area has a boss guarding the way north. Beat them to earn five badges:

  | Area | Boss | Badge |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Route 1 (meadow) | Ranger Rowan | Sum Badge |
  | Sunny Shore (beach) | Captain Marina | Wave Badge |
  | Dusty Canyon (desert) | Sheriff Sol | Mesa Badge |
  | Frostpeak (snow) | Coach Ivy | Frost Badge |
  | Glowcap Cave (crystal cave) | Keeper Onyx | Crystal Badge |

  Every boss opens with the Legend for the skill you need most. Wild Legends get stronger in each new area.
- **137 Legends:** 3 buddies, 30 in Pinecrest Valley, 20 in each of the other four worlds, 18 grade ultra rares, 5 world Legends and 1 mythical. Every area has a Legend for adding, taking away, numbers, groups and sharing, plus a boss partner.
- **Map:** MENU → Map shows the world and lets you quick-travel to any place you've visited.
- **Worlds and the Portal Shrine:** Pinecrest Valley is World 1 (Route 1 to Glowcap Cave). Beating Keeper Onyx wakes a portal to World 2, and the Portal Shrine, a ring of standing stones down the path west of Pinecrest Town, has a portal to all five worlds. Sleeping portals are dark until the world before them is beaten. Every world's first area has a portal home to the shrine.
- **World 2, Whisperwood:** Whisper Forest, Mirror River, Bogwater Swamp, Treetop Trail and Thunder Falls, with 20 new Legends and five bosses (Warden Fern, Fisher Finn, Marsh Mae, Scout Wren, Elder Brook). A portal by the forest entrance goes back to the shrine.

  | Puzzles | Whisper Forest | Mirror River | Bogwater Swamp | Treetop Trail | Thunder Falls |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Adding | Plusquirrel | Otteradd | Toadtotal | Plusquirrel | Otteradd |
  | Taking away | Mothaway | Beaverbite | Snailess | Mothaway | Snailess |
  | Numbers | Mossbig | Heronmore | Heronmore | Toucount | Mossbig |
  | Groups | Mushgroup | Dragonfleet | Dragonfleet | Chimpairs | Chimpairs |
  | Sharing | Hedgeshare | Lilysplit | Lilysplit | Hedgeshare | Mistsplit |
  | Boss partner | Oakulus | Hippomath | Crocalc | Owlgebra | Cascoi |
- **Rare Legends (228 Legends in all, counting evolved forms):**
  - *Ultra rares:* 18, three for each grade. Each waits at a fixed spot and appears only while playing its grade, once the player is getting strong at its skill (82% or better over at least 6 tries). For example, Glimmerdot on Route 1 shows up for Kindergarten counters, and Griffinity on Treetop Trail for Grade 5 two-digit multipliers. The Legendex says which skill wakes each one.
  - *World Legends:* Equalion waits in Glowcap Cave after the Crystal Badge, and Whisperstag at Thunder Falls after the Cascade Badge.
  - *Golden Legends:* answer 10 puzzles in a row on the first try and the next wild Legend is golden. Golden friends stay gold in your team and the Legendex.
  - *The mythical:* Infinix appears in the stone circle once every boss of a world has been beaten at every grade (K–5).
  - Rare and golden Legends always want to be friends once calmed. If you say no or run, they wait right where they were.
- **Worlds 3–5:**
  - *Brightport City:* Sunny Park, Rooftop Run, Subway Station, Harbor Docks, Clock Tower Square. Bosses: Gardener Rose, Courier Kai, Conductor Lou, Skipper Jo, Mayor Bell. World Legend: Brightwing.
  - *Coral Deep:* Rainbow Reef, Kelp Forest, Sunken Ship, Dark Trench, Sunken Temple. Bosses: Diver Dot, Ranger Kip, Captain Barnacle, Explorer Nyx, Oracle Pearl. World Legend: Leviathorn.
  - *Sky Isles:* Cloud Meadow, Windmill Isle, Rainbow Bridge, Storm Peak, Sky Castle. Bosses: Shepherd Sky, Miller Mo, Painter Iris, Captain Bolt, Queen Aria. World Legend: Celestail. Beating Queen Aria finishes the journey, and a portal by the castle leads home.
  - Each world has 20 Legends of its own (15 wild, 5 boss partners). Wild Legends get stronger world by world.
- **Battles:** the two Legends face each other, bounce on their toes and blink now and then.
- **Boss houses:** every boss guards the door of a log cabin, striped tent, stone tower or domed hall. Rest Centers have a heart on the roof, Professor Pi's lab has a π badge, and your home has a teal roof and a chimney.
- **Badge colors:** each badge's color shows the grade it was won at: Bronze (K), Silver (1), Gold (2), Emerald (3), Sapphire (4), Crystal (5). Start a harder grade and every boss offers a rematch to upgrade its badge.
- **Legend Terminal:** when your team is full (6), new friends wait in storage. Use the terminal in Professor Pi's lab or any Rest Center to take them out, put team members away, or swap. Your buddy always stays with you.
- **Evolution:**
  - *Buddies* grow twice, at level 16 and level 36: Sproutle → Sprigleaf → Grovegiant, Emberkit → Blazecat → Sunmane, Splashfin → Surfin → Tidalfin.
  - *Wild Legends* (all 85) grow once. A Legend is ready at its home area's level + 8 (Hootsum at 10, Sailsplit at 98), but it only evolves once the player is "Getting strong" at that Legend's kind of math (any skill of that family at their grade: 82%+ over 6 tries). Until then it glows and says what to practice. Learning is what makes Legends grow.
  - Evolution is checked after each battle you don't lose and after practicing with your buddy. Evolved Legends are drawn bigger, with a crest, wings and a gem in their math family's color, and get more HP and power. Golden Legends stay golden.
  - Boss partners and rare Legends don't evolve.
- **Remy, your rival:** Professor Pi's grandkid, with the buddy after yours (Sproutle → Emberkit → Splashfin → Sproutle). Remy walks in from off screen (from the way to the next area) a few steps into Route 1, Frostpeak, Bogwater Swamp, Subway Station, Sunken Ship and Rainbow Bridge, with a team that grows from 1 to 5 Legends (a little stronger than the area's wild Legends; Remy's buddy evolves too). After you win, Remy heads off toward the next area. Lose, and Remy is waiting outside the Rest Center when you come out: say yes for a rematch with your rested team, or no and he goes on ahead (you'll meet him at his next stop).
- **Your room and the Badge Case:** take the stairs in your house up to your room. The Badge Case shows all 25 badges, a row per world, each in the color of the grade it was won at.
- The Rest Center (pink roof) heals your team. If your whole team needs a rest, you wake up there. Nothing is lost.

**MENU in battle:** at the SOLVE / TEAM / RUN choice, MENU opens Team, Legendex, Grown-ups and Settings. Map and Save & load aren't available mid-battle, because a save holds your team and where you're standing, not the battle itself.

**Controls:** on-screen D-pad, A, B and MENU buttons, or the keyboard: arrows/WASD to move, Z or Space for A, X for B, Enter for the menu. During a question, type the number and press Enter (or 1–4 to pick a choice), and press B or H for a hint.

**Game controllers and handhelds** (Retroid Pocket, Bluetooth gamepads): press any button and the on-screen controls go away so the game fills the screen (or open the game with `?handheld`, or pick MENU → Settings → Controls → Controller). D-pad moves, A and B as usual, Select opens the menu and Start opens the map (on the Retroid Pocket Classic: the top and lower of the two small buttons). During a typed answer the number keypad sits beside the screen: the D-pad moves the gold cursor, A presses the key, B or Z erases, Start or C checks the answer (on the Retroid the check key is blue, like C), and X or Y asks for a hint. If A and B come out swapped (or a button does nothing), open `tools/pad-test.html` on the device: it shows each button's number and can swap A/B in the game's map (`mql:padmap` in browser storage).

## Hints

The buddy helps the way Math Quest's tutor does:

1. **💡 Hint:** a short strategy tip ("Make a ten!", "Think addition: 7 + ? = 15") and a picture: ten-frame, base-ten blocks, array, number line or fraction bars.
2. **A second try:** after a wrong answer, a hint about the likely mistake ("Careful! This one is taking away, not adding.", "Did you remember to borrow?").
3. **🦉 Teach me:** a step-by-step lesson (count on, make a ten, count back, column adding, borrowing, skip counting, friendly fives, think multiplication, rounding on a number line, split and multiply, long division, matching fraction bottoms, lining up decimal points and more). From Grade 1 the buddy then asks "Which trick did we use?", and a similar problem follows to try yourself. After a kid has seen a lesson twice, it skips the first worked step.

## The math

Skills, number ranges and difficulty follow Math Quest's grade scheme (Common Core K–5). Each grade has all five kinds of Legend math:

| Grade | Adding | Taking away | Numbers | Groups | Sharing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kindergarten | within 10 | within 10 | counting, which is more | counting on, by 10s | parts of a number (5 = 2 + ?) |
| Grade 1 | within 20 | within 20 | comparing, tens and ones | doubles | mystery numbers (6 + ? = 13) |
| Grade 2 | two-digit | two-digit | place value, skip counting | equal groups | halves |
| Grade 3 | within 1,000 | within 1,000 | rounding | times tables | division facts |
| Grade 4 | to 10,000 | to 10,000 | rounding big numbers | multi-digit × one digit | dividing by one digit, equal fractions |
| Grade 5 | decimals | decimals | decimal place value | two-digit × two-digit | long division, adding fractions |

Also from Math Quest: weak skills come up more often (each skill's record is halved at 20 tries, so recent tries count most and an early struggle fades), mastered skills stretch to bigger numbers, 15% of problems review earlier grades, Grades 2–4 include short story problems, and missed problems come back after 1, 3 and 7 days.

**Research behind it:** typed answers use retrieval practice; review after 1, 3 and 7 days is spaced practice; wrong-answer hints give specific feedback; pictures (ten-frames, blocks, arrays, number lines, fraction bars) come before symbols; Teach me uses worked examples that fade as a kid gets familiar; number lines build number sense (Siegler & Booth); asking which trick was used prompts self-explanation; and praise names the strategy that worked, not the child ("Making a ten worked!").

On top of that, wild encounters follow the player's needs: while a skill is a real struggle lately (under 70% right on the first try over its last 10 tries), 40% of encounters bring the Legend that practices it. As the skill improves, that Legend shows up at higher levels with bigger numbers.

## For grown-ups

- **Grade:** set at the start and changeable any time in MENU → Settings.
- **Read aloud:** off by default. Turn it on in MENU → Settings to have the game read dialog, questions and hints out loud. The 🔊 button reads the current question at any time.
- **Reading speed:** MENU → Settings → Reading speed: Slower, Normal or Faster. It's saved with the game.
- **Voice:** the game picks the most natural-sounding voice on the device, and MENU → Settings → Voice lets you choose another. For the best sound, install a high-quality voice:
  - iPhone/iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, then download a voice marked "Enhanced" or "Premium" (for example Ava or Zoe).
  - Mac: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices, then download a Premium or Enhanced voice.
  - Windows: Microsoft Edge has natural-sounding online voices built in (for example Aria or Jenny).
  - Chrome on any computer includes Google voices, which sound better than the basic system ones.
- **Progress:** MENU → Grown-ups shows first-try accuracy and mastery for each skill, which skill is being practiced most, and how many missed problems are waiting for review.
- **Progress report:** `progress.html` (linked from MENU → Grown-ups, and a tile on the Retroid's grown-up shelf) reads every game on the device and explains it in words: strengths, what needs practice (with missed problems and an idea to try at home), skills that are clicking or slipping, the kinds of mistakes made (added instead of taking away, off by one, carrying, digits swapped…), and the last two weeks of play. Wrong answers and days played are recorded from rev 49 on.
- Progress is saved on the device (browser storage). Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Several games:** the title screen lists every game on the device, labeled with the player's name and starter Legend ("Mia · Sproutle", with a number if two match), last played first. New Game adds a game without erasing any; Erase a game asks first (and on the Retroid, Homebase also asks for the grown-up PIN). MENU → Save & load → Switch game goes back to the list.
- **Save files:** MENU → Save & load → Export save file downloads (or shares) a backup. Load save file adds it as another game, on any device, and the title screen has Load save file too.
- **Devices:** works on phones, tablets and computers. On iPad and other tablets it scales up. Turned sideways, the controls move to a grip on each side of the screen, like a Nintendo Switch. MENU → Settings → Controls can keep them on the sides or below the screen all the time (this setting stays on the device, not in save files).

## Running it

It's a plain static site with no build step. Serve the folder and open it in a browser:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then go to http://localhost:8000. It also works offline after the first visit, and it can be added to a phone's home screen.

To publish it, push to GitHub and turn on GitHub Pages for the `main` branch (Settings → Pages).

## Files

- `index.html`, `style.css`: the page and the handheld-console look
- `progress.html`: the grown-ups' progress report (reads the saves; uses `js/data.js` and `js/math.js`)
- `js/data.js`: creatures, moves, people and maps
- `js/art.js`: all pixel art, drawn in code
- `js/math.js`: the math engine: skills per grade, adapting, hints and Teach me lessons
- `js/game.js`: movement, the following buddy, dialog, menus, battles and saving
- `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `*.png`: offline support and app icons
- `tools/icons.html`: draws the home screen icon options from the game's art
- `tools/qc.html`: quality checks for Legends, maps, math problems, hints, reading level and offline files
- `js/version.js`: the "rev" shown under the controls. A git hook (`tools/git-hooks/pre-commit`) stamps the next revision number and date on every commit. Install it once per clone: `cp tools/git-hooks/pre-commit .git/hooks/`
- `tools/worldgen/`: builds worlds 3–5. Each area's layout is generated from a fixed seed (same map for everyone), with its own size, winding path and features from `shapes.py`, and is checked for reachability before it's kept
- `tools/layout-qc.js`, `tools/flow-qc.js`: in-game checks for question layout and the hint / Teach me path
- `tools/pad-test.html`: shows what a game controller sends (button numbers, axes, key events) and fixes the button map

**Quality checks:** serve the folder, open `tools/qc.html` and read the report (it runs about 33,000 checks). For the in-game checks, open the game with `?dev`, then in the browser console run `await import('./tools/layout-qc.js'); await layoutQC()` (try phone and tablet sizes) or `await import('./tools/flow-qc.js'); await flowQC()`.

**Dev unlock:** open the game with `?dev` in the address (for example https://samw90.github.io/MathQuestLegends/?dev). MENU → Settings then shows *Dev: unlock everything* (all badges, places, Legends and portals, plus a level 30 team) and *Dev: go to Portal Shrine*. Unlocking changes the save, so export it first to keep a copy.

For testing, open the game with `?dev` in the address to get `MQL.ask(MQL.MathEngine.make('sub-100', 2))` in the browser console, or `?dev&quiz=sub-100&grade=2` to jump straight into practice questions (handy on a phone or simulator).
